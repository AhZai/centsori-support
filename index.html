Centsori helps you record expenses, create categories with your own emoji and colors, and plan upcoming recurring payments.

Contact support

Email ahzai520@icloud.com for help, bug reports, or privacy requests. Include your iOS version and a description of the problem. Do not email card details or financial backups unless specifically needed and agreed.

Add a transaction

Open Overview, tap +, enter the amount and merchant, choose a category, then save. Choose Put later to keep a valid transaction pending categorization.

Connect with Shortcuts

Open Settings → Connect with Shortcuts and follow all five steps. In Shortcuts, create a Transaction automation for your Wallet cards, select Run Immediately, and add Centsori’s Add transaction action. Connect available Amount, Merchant, and Currency code values from Shortcut Input. Review every draft before saving.

Availability depends on iOS, card, and region. A Wallet tap does not prove a payment succeeded or settled. Centsori cannot create your personal automation silently. Manual entry remains available.

iCloud backup

Use the same Apple Account with iCloud enabled. Check Settings → iCloud and backup for status. If two copies differ, choose carefully and keep a recovery copy. Exported JSON backups are an optional additional backup.

Recurring payments

Add subscriptions or instalments under Bills. Forecasts show upcoming costs; they do not charge you or automatically create expense records.

Currency and languages

New entries use your device’s region currency. Imported or Shortcut entries retain their supplied currency. Amounts are not converted using exchange rates. English, Bahasa Melayu, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese are supported.
