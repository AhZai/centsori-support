Centsori Support

Privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026

Centsori is provided by Lan Chee Keong. Contact ahzai520@icloud.com for support or privacy questions.

Your ledger

The app stores transactions, merchant names, amounts, currencies, dates, notes, categories, recurring payment plans, merchant rules, and preferences on your device. The developer does not receive or have access to your ledger through the app. No advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or tracking code is included.

Private iCloud storage

When iCloud is available, Centsori backs up your ledger to the private CloudKit database in your Apple Account. Apple provides and processes this storage under its own terms and privacy policy. Recovery depends on using the same Apple Account, available iCloud storage, a successful backup, and the backup still existing. The developer cannot restore your private iCloud records for you.

Shortcuts and Wallet

If you configure a personal Shortcuts automation, the app receives transaction details you choose to pass to it, such as amount, merchant, and currency. The app does not read your complete Wallet history, card number, security code, or banking login. Review details before saving; a Wallet tap is not proof of payment settlement.

Exports and recovery copies

JSON exports contain readable financial information. You control where they are saved or shared. The app may keep local recovery copies before replacing ledger data. Deleting a transaction from the current ledger may not erase earlier exports or recovery copies.

Retention and deletion

Your data is retained until you remove it. To remove all copies, delete applicable local app data, recovery files, exported backups, and Centsori data in iCloud using your device’s iCloud storage controls. Deleting the app alone does not delete the private iCloud backup, and reinstalling can restore it. Do not reopen the app with an older local copy after deleting iCloud data if you intend to remove all copies.

Support requests

If you email support, we receive your email address and the content you send. We use it to respond and investigate the issue, retain it only as needed for support and legal obligations, and do not sell it. Please avoid sending unnecessary financial information.

This website

This support site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement. No additional analytics or advertising scripts are added.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app behavior changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
